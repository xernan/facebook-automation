Owner rules on which cards count as a bank credit card (read by the AI on every run; edit freely):
- Counts without asking: a credit card from BDO, BPI, Metrobank, RCBC, EastWest, China Bank, PNB, Security Bank, UnionBank (and old Citi cards), HSBC, AUB, Maybank, PSBank.
- Confirm first: when they name a bank or app most people know for ATM, debit or savings (Landbank, DBP, Maya, GoTyme, CIMB, Tonik, SeaBank, Bank of Commerce, any rural or digital bank), or any name not listed here: WebSearch "<name> credit card Philippines" to see if a credit card exists, then ask them once with fb_send_message in plain words. Owner: many people only see the difference when asked about ATM, so ask like this: "ATM card ba yan (pang-withdraw ng sarili mong pera)? O credit card (may credit limit at monthly bill na binabayaran mo)?" Then and call fb_flow_answer kind="asked". Pass it in cards only after they say yes.
- Maya: only a Maya credit card counts. A Maya debit card or the Maya wallet does not.
- Does not count: Atome, any debit or ATM card, GCash or GCredit, pay-later accounts (BillEase, SPayLater, Lazada PayLater), loan accounts.
- Home Credit: a loan or pay-later account does not count. If they say it is a Home Credit credit card, WebSearch and confirm as above.
- Owner tip: many clients do not know the difference between a credit card and a debit card. Whenever a card sounds doubtful, ask whether it is an ATM card first.
